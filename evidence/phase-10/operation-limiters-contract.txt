  expiry and strict input/reply validation.
- Missing, malformed, wrong-type, future, non-expiring and excessive-TTL state
  is recovered only at the exact limiter key.
- Engagement partitions progress and watchlist policy by authorized account
  pseudonym and operation after current owner plus idempotent replay checks.
- A bounded/cancellable same-key lane serializes receipt inspection and rate
  admission. Five concurrent progress retries and five concurrent watchlist
  retries each consume one admission and produce/replay one durable effect.
- A 1,024-partition local shield bounds degraded admission and sheds a local hot
  caller before another Redis command. Progress refills at four tokens/second;
  watchlist refills at one token/second.
- Redis failure cannot acknowledge a mutation. Existing PostgreSQL transaction,
  receipt and outbox behavior remains authoritative.

  source call. Home rails bypass that lane; cancellation and shutdown settle

  account/profile IDs, pseudonyms, credentials and Redis keys are not labels.
