Covered contract
- One bounded Redis-server-time Lua decision uses integer milli-tokens, finite
  expiry and strict input/reply validation.
- Missing, malformed, wrong-type, future, non-expiring and excessive-TTL state
  is recovered only at the exact limiter key.
- Engagement partitions progress and watchlist policy by authorized account
  pseudonym and operation after current owner plus idempotent replay checks.
- A bounded/cancellable same-key lane serializes receipt inspection and rate
  admission. Five concurrent progress retries and five concurrent watchlist
  retries each consume one admission and produce/replay one durable effect.
- The application derives the same bounded admission digest in separate recorder
  instances. The Redis command atomically stores its finite marker only after an
  allowed charge; a second adapter reuses that decision. Distinct admission
  digests still consume or reject against the shared account-operation bucket.
- The real PostgreSQL fixture now holds two recorder replicas after negative
  receipt reads and requires two limiter calls, one limiter charge, one receipt,
  one event and one durable progress result.
- A 1,024-partition local shield bounds degraded admission and sheds a local hot
  caller before another Redis command. Progress refills at four tokens/second;
  watchlist refills at one token/second.
- Redis failure cannot acknowledge a mutation. Existing PostgreSQL transaction,
  receipt and outbox behavior remains authoritative.
- Discovery search admits two active operations and one FIFO waiter for at most
  100 milliseconds. Overflow is a portable `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` payload with no
  source call. Home rails bypass that lane; cancellation and shutdown settle
  waiters and release permits.
- Metrics use only finite limiter, operation, outcome and queue buckets. Raw
  account/profile IDs, pseudonyms, credentials and Redis keys are not labels.
- Federation output additively exposes limit enum values and nullable Engagement
  retry metadata. The versioned first-party operation inventory selects the new
  retry fields.
