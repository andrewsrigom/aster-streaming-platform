  with bounded backoff, exposes an exact rebuild barrier and owns shutdown;
- Catalog export uses its exact purpose-separated operation, credential and
  endpoint with a shared one-active/no-queue lane, two-second deadline, no
  retry/redirect and a 64-KiB response cap;
- export pages admit at most two strictly ascending title snapshots and reject
  malformed cursors, arrays or envelopes before projection;
- rebuild captures a broker barrier, resumes a durable Catalog keyset scan into
  the BUILDING generation, checkpoints each page and promotes only after the
  scan and consumer catch-up complete;
- partial source/projector/store failure preserves the active generation and
  resumable building checkpoint;
- consumer next offsets are durably recorded on the active generation and any
  concurrent building generation before Kafka acknowledgement; a new rebuild
  copies active progress, closing the barrier/start race without skipping facts;
- scan checkpoints cannot overwrite concurrently advancing consumer offsets.
