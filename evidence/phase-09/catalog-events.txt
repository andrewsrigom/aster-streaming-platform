Implemented scope:
- Catalog v1 records are bounded, copied and validated before parsing;
- the event is a refresh hint only; searchable metadata always comes from a
  fresh Catalog-owned snapshot;
- the fixed HTTP owner client uses the purpose-separated credential, exact
  operation, 64 KiB response cap, two-second deadline, one active read and no retry;
- the broker handler acknowledges only applied, duplicate or durably quarantined
  outcomes and logs bounded identifiers without event bytes or title metadata;
- one in-process event is admitted without a queue, with caller cancellation;
- duplicate/stale delivery is reconciled through durable source-version fences;
- bounded poison records are quarantined before acknowledgement;
- oversized, unavailable, indeterminate, cancelled or full-quarantine outcomes
  leave the broker offset retryable;
- quarantine stores exact key/value/header bytes in 128 serialized slots;
- replay removes the exact record only after durable apply or idempotent success;
- the projector role has function-only recovery access and no direct table access;
- migration 0002 is additive and its down migration refuses non-empty recovery data.
