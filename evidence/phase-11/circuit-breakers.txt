## Deterministic behavior

- Policy: 30-second rolling window, maximum 64 logical outcomes, minimum four
  samples, 50% failure threshold and five-second open interval.
- Playback loopback: owner failures opened the breaker; the following logical
  call was rejected without HTTP. After cooldown, exactly one held half-open
  probe reached Catalog while a concurrent call was rejected; success closed
  the breaker and the next call completed normally.
- The default Playback policy opened after four failed owner requests and
  suppressed the fifth logical call.
- Three invalid Playback publications (mismatched identity, stale observation
  and invalid delivery URL) opened the focused breaker; the next call made no
  HTTP request.
- Discovery snapshot failures opened only the snapshot breaker; an independent
  export operation still reached Catalog and completed.
- Mismatched, stale and malformed Discovery snapshots opened only the snapshot
  breaker. A malformed export snapshot opened only the export breaker, and each
  following call was suppressed without HTTP.
- A complete safe read, including its bounded retry, contributes one outcome.
  Caller cancellation is ignored; malformed, unavailable, timeout and permanent
  owner failures count as failures.
- Telemetry emitted only the fixed dependency, operation, state and event
  vocabularies defined by the contract.

## Interpretation and limits

The candidate stops repeated pressure on an unhealthy Catalog operation while
preserving authority: Playback remains fail closed and Discovery creates no
owner data. Breaker state is deliberately process-local and resets on restart.
Threshold calibration, controlled game days and operational runbooks remain
later Phase 11 work. No public failure-injection path exists.
