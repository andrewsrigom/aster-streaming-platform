Interpretation
Selected transient reads recover once while remaining inside one logical client
permit. Permanent owner data/protocol failures, attempt timeout, cancellation and
insufficient remaining budget never start another request. The fixed concrete
25 ms ceiling yields a 13-25 ms equal-jitter delay; injected zero randomness in
loopback tests exercises its 13 ms lower bound deterministically.

Limitations
- Individual test durations are observed runner times, not latency benchmarks or
  SLOs. Deterministic delay assertions establish policy math independently of
  scheduler noise.
- The loopback fixture proves Node HTTP cleanup and classification on one local
  environment. Protected CI remains required at the published exact head.
- Router and Apollo Client retain one attempt; this candidate introduces no
  mutation retry, circuit breaker or production failure-injection endpoint.
