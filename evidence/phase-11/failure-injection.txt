## Implemented boundary

`tools/failure-lab.ts` provides two development adapters:

1. one HTTP scenario fixed at construction and bound only to `127.0.0.1`;
2. exactly-two sequential deliveries of the same synthetic event reference.

Both accept only `local` or `integration`. `production` fails before a server or
delivery exists. No app, service, worker or package imports the tool, and a
source-tree contract test preserves that structural isolation. There is no
environment-variable switch, product route, remote bind option or request field
that selects a fault.

Finite policy bounds:

- delay: 1–10,000 ms;
- terminal hold: 1–30,000 ms;
- active saturation slots: 1–16;
- injected activations: 1–100;
- configured response: at most 16,384 UTF-8 bytes;
- selected status: 429, 500, 502, 503 or 504 for the error mode only.

Every response capable of carrying headers includes fixed injection, mode and
scenario headers. Reset/partial-stream outcomes and duplicate deliveries expose
the same `aster.failure_injection` tag through finite observations. Observer
failure is ignored and cannot change the injected result.

## Interpretation and limits

The current candidate makes failure mechanics reproducible without exposing a
chaos endpoint or production switch. It does not claim a Redis, broker,
database, media-worker or complete service game day. Those experiments will use
owner-specific controls and retained product assertions in the next Phase 11
item. Shared-host timing proves ordering and bounded termination only; it is not
a latency SLO or capacity benchmark.
