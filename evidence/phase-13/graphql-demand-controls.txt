Implemented contract
- All five owners link Federation v2.9 and publish standard `@cost` and
  `@listSize` metadata for the fields reachable from current trusted operations.
- Root work has an explicit owner weight. Every reachable list has a finite
  owner-backed assumed maximum; literal slices may lower but never raise that
  maximum, while variables conservatively use it.
- Every selected non-root field on a cost-owned entity type has a direct field
  weight. Removing one fails generation rather than falling back implicitly.
- Composition parses at most 2,000 tokens, validates the exact body and SHA-256,
  expands fragments under finite cycle/count limits and calculates aliases,
  depth, root fields, selections, maximum nested list expansion and static cost.
- Invalid/missing metadata, an excessive literal, numeric overflow, a stale
  artifact or any operation above policy fails generation before image delivery.
- Hosted admission remains the exact analyzed trusted name/hash union. The
  schema delivery index remains current-only during a bounded retained overlap.
- The generated manifest is packaged in the Router image and contains reviewed
  names, hashes and finite numeric profiles, never variables or request data.
- CI packages the pinned Router and must accept canonical Browse while rejecting
  parser-token, body-size, batching and introspection probes with bounded,
  sanitized responses before the existing trusted-operation/runtime journey.
