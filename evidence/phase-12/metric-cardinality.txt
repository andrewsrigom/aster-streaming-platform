## Finite series budget

The OpenTelemetry SDK limit remains configurable from 16 to 512 series per
instrument and defaults to 128. Overflow is aggregated into the SDK's explicit
overflow series rather than creating unbounded labels.

| Instrument family | Finite dimensions | Maximum authored combinations |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Node memory | four memory types | 4 |
| PostgreSQL pool | five roles x three lifecycle states x five connection states | 75 |
| Event outcome | three owners x two stages x six outcomes | 36 |
| Event age | same finite outcome dimensions | 36 |
| Product outcome | four operations x nine outcomes | 36 |
| Product duration | same finite product dimensions | 36 |

Pool roles are `primary`, `projection`, `relay`, `consumer` and `operator`.
Event owners are `identity`, `catalog` and `engagement`; stages are `publish`
and `consume`. Numeric pool values do not become labels.

## Boundary validation

- exact own data properties are required; unknown keys and accessor-backed
  observations are rejected without invoking getters;
- pool counts must be safe integers within maximum 1–128, and idle cannot exceed
  total;
- event age must be finite, non-negative and no more than seven days; producers
  and consumers omit future or excessive age without losing the finite outcome;
- product duration must be finite, non-negative and no more than 300 seconds;
- an invalid observation increments only the finite `invalid_dimension` drop
  reason and cannot alter product work.

The contract test injects a private-looking pool label, excessive event age and
infinite product duration. All three are rejected, the drop count increases by
three, and collected points contain none of the canaries
`private-secret`, `profile-id`, `title-id`, `request-id`, `trace-id`, HTTP URLs
or SQL text. A separate test drives more HTTP combinations than a 16-series
limit and observes the explicit overflow series.

## Prohibited labels

Account, profile, title, event, request, trace, publication and media identifiers;
credentials; endpoints; full URLs; SQL; GraphQL documents; exception messages;
and signed media URLs are absent from the new metric attributes. Correlation
belongs in bounded logs and sampled traces, not metrics.
