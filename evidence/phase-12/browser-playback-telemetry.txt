## Contract

- Local attempt sampling is 100%; remote attempt sampling is 0%.
- One player attempt retains at most 64 finite events and one bounded summary in
  JavaScript memory only.
- Retry and player unmount synchronously erase the recorder; late mutations are
  ignored.
- First-frame success requires a decoded-frame callback or the bounded advancing
  media-time fallback.
- Rebuffering starts only after first frame, excludes pause/seek and ends on
  playing or fatal failure.
- The report contains no account, profile, title, session, request, trace,
  publication or media identifiers; no URL, token, cookie, manifest, arbitrary
  label or error message is accepted.
- No browser exporter, ingestion route, durable storage or server retention is
  present. This evidence makes no field-SLI or capacity claim.
