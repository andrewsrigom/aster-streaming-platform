Requirement and policy
----------------------

P12-R07 requires multi-window burn-rate or equivalent alerts linked to
runbooks. ADR-0043 defines:

- rapid page intent for supergraph, Catalog title read and playback start:
  14.4x over 1h/5m or 6x over 6h/30m;
- sustained working-hours ticket intent for all four released SLIs:
  3x over 1d/2h or 1x over 3d/6h;
- both windows in a pair above the threshold derived from the owning error
  budget;
- complete sampled coverage of every requested window before its ratio exists;
- seven possible alert instances with static owner, response, impact,
  confirmation, dashboard and runbook metadata;
- no external receiver or notification-delivery claim.

The local Prometheus time ceiling becomes three days while the 128 MB size cap,
two-second query timeout, two-query concurrency, 10,000-sample ceiling,
loopback listener and 256 MiB process limit remain unchanged. The effective
retention is whichever time/size limit wins.

container changed. Final teardown removed the exact probe container and image
tag. Post-cleanup queries returned zero matching resources; existing projects
were untouched.

Workloads and interpretation
----------------------------

- A 2% one-hour bad ratio fires rapid alerts for the three page-class SLIs and
  proves progress write does not become an interrupting page.
- A burned long window followed by clean 5m/30m short windows produces no rapid
  alert, proving both windows are required.
- Twenty minutes of total failure creates no alert because neither long window
  has complete sampled coverage.
- A rapid alert returns inactive after its five-minute short pair clears while
  its one-hour history remains burned.
- A 0.12% three-day bad ratio fires sustained alerts for all four SLIs, including
  the smaller 0.05% progress-write budget.
- Sustained alert state returns inactive after the six-hour short window clears
  while the three-day history remains burned.
- No traffic and an unreviewed/excluded SLI value create no alert.

Navigation and response
-----------------------

Every alert links to the immutable operational overview and
RUNBOOKS.md#runbook-critical-journey-slo-burn. The runbook provides trigger,
owner/impact mapping, exact rapid/sustained confirmation queries, immediate
mitigation, diagnosis by SLI, recovery, rollback, escalation and follow-up
evidence. It explicitly refuses authorization, rights, durability or idempotency
bypasses.

Limitations
-----------

- Synthetic time series prove rule behavior, not field reliability, incident
  notification or operator response time.
- Five-minute rate integration adds up to one recording-window of boundary
  smoothing.
- A fresh store or a store evicted by the 128 MB cap keeps the affected ratio
  and alert absent until complete sampled coverage exists.
- The local profile has no Alertmanager, receiver, credential, real on-call
  person or schedule.
- No 28/30-day representative dataset exists, so no historical SLO compliance
  result is asserted.
- Protected packaged-Prometheus rules/alerts API acceptance and exact-head
  review remain pending.
