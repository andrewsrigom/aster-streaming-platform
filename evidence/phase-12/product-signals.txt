## Implemented contract

Two finite instruments record duration and outcome:

- `aster.product.operation.duration` in seconds;
- `aster.product.operation.outcomes` as a counter.

The only operation labels are `playback_session`, `progress_write`,
`media_processing` and `media_publication`. The only result labels are
`completed`, `stale`, `conflict`, `not_playable`, `rejected`, `cancelled`,
`unavailable`, `indeterminate` and `failed`.

Playback session creation and progress acceptance are wrapped at their existing
application-composition boundary. Every finite owner result maps to one metric
outcome, while an exception maps to `failed` and is rethrown unchanged. Media
processing observes claim, retained-candidate and completion outcomes around the
existing isolated worker span. The one-shot publication CLI configures the
reviewed OTLP exporter, records its final result and performs a bounded flush
without changing publication or recovery status.

Existing cache duration/outcome/payload instruments remain the cache-
effectiveness source; this slice does not duplicate them. Existing local player
state still measures first frame and rebuffering for the demo, but remote browser
collection remains explicitly deferred until P12-R11 owns sampling, privacy,
transport and retention. Therefore this candidate makes no field-QoE claim.

## Limits

The metrics describe backend product decisions, not viewer-perceived playback
quality. No account, profile, title, request, trace, publication, media object,
GraphQL document or signed URL becomes a metric label. Dashboard queries, SLI
populations, SLOs and alerts remain P12-R05 through P12-R07/R12.
