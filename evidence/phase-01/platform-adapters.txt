PostgreSQL implemented contract
- @aster/postgres accepts only a bounded plain own-data configuration, validates a PostgreSQL URL without reflecting it, caps the pool at 32 connections, and applies finite connection, idle, statement, query, operation, and close deadlines.
- The public slice exposes connect, a fixed SELECT 1 AS aster_probe probe, a bounded process-local pool snapshot, stable sanitized results, one shared close operation with caller-local cancellation, and lifecycle hooks compatible with @aster/runtime. It exposes no general SQL function, query builder, migration, product repository, schema, transaction policy, or credential-bearing error.
- Adapter-owned capacity prevents an unbounded vendor wait queue. A timed-out or cancelled acquisition keeps its slot reserved until the eventual client is destroyed or acquisition fails. Probe cancellation and timeout destroy the client before releasing the slot.
- Dependency telemetry uses only the released finite postgresql/connect and postgresql/probe vocabulary and outcomes. Snapshot counts are bounded process-local observations; no backend metric, dashboard, or SLO is claimed.
- Close rejects an already-aborted owner without starting shutdown, destroys active owned clients, waits settled adapter work, calls pool.end once, returns within its configured caller deadline, and may remain visibly closing until the underlying vendor drain finishes.
