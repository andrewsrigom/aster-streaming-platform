Deadline checkpoint
- Added packages/runtime/src/deadline.ts and the public @aster/runtime export with no new package, dependency, lockfile, service, route, schema, durable state, or vendor type.
- The accepted timeout is one integer millisecond through 300000 milliseconds. One monotonic budget produces a derived AbortSignal and non-increasing remaining value. Parent cancellation never copies its reason.
- Expiry, parent cancellation, and explicit disposal release the timer and parent listener once. Disposal does not abort completed work. The default timer is unreferenced and cannot keep an otherwise idle Node.js process alive.
- Plain own-data validation rejects unknown keys, accessors, throwing proxies, forged signals, and invalid budgets with bounded cause-free issues. Built-in AbortSignal/EventTarget operations bypass caller-owned signal properties.
- Sixteen focused deadline cases cover deterministic expiry, regression-resistant remaining budget, live and already-aborted parents, hostile signal properties, disposal, timeout bounds, hostile option shapes, forged signals, throwing/synchronous/invalid schedulers, late clock failure, real expiry, natural subprocess exit, and declaration isolation.
